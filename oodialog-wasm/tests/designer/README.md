# Programs written by the ooDialog Designer

`Calculator`, `Contacts` and `Gadgets` are the generated programs of
ooDialog Designer v0.3.0 (architecture: Tom Dyer; code: Claude (Opus 5.5)),
copied unchanged with their `.steps` and `.expect`. They are kept here as a
contract test: a change to ooDialog/WASM must not break what the designer
writes. Do not edit them here; take new copies from the designer.

    python3 tools/oodtest.py tests/designer/*.rex
